Chicken Dum Biryani

    The house promise

    Sealed with dough. Opened at the table, never before. The handi travels eight feet from fire to you, and the first breath of steam is yours.

    The method

    Five steps. No shortcuts.

    STEP 01

    Marinate overnight

    Chicken sits twelve hours in hung curd, browned onion, ginger-garlic and our garam masala. Nothing rushed, nothing powdered fresh.

    STEP 02

    Parboil the rice

    Aged basmati boiled to exactly seventy percent in spiced water - kewra, bay, shah jeera - then drained and rested.

    STEP 03

    Layer by hand

    Rice over chicken, saffron milk over rice, ghee, mint, fried onion. Three layers, always in that order, always by hand.

    STEP 04

    Seal the handi

    A ring of wheat dough pressed around the rim. The lid locks, the steam stays in, the dum begins. Twenty-two minutes on low flame.

    STEP 05

    Lift at the table

    The seal cracks only in front of you. That first cloud of steam took ninety minutes to make and one second to meet you.

        House rules

        1. The handi opens at your tableNever in the kitchen, never on the way. If the seal is broken, the handi goes back and a new one is made.
        2. No biryani past tenWhen the day's handis are done, they are done. Tomorrow's marination has already begun.
        3. Spices are toasted, never bought groundEleven whole spices, one iron tawa, every single morning since the first day.
        4. Eat with your handsCutlery is available and nobody will judge you. But the biryani tastes better the old way.
